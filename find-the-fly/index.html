Find the Fly

One buzz. One room. Zero mercy.

Scan real living messes. Think where a fly would land — light, food, warmth — then pin it before the swarm.

How it works

  1. Scan the room. Every scene is a lived-in space with crumbs, lamps, vents, and trash.
  2. Think like a fly. Warm rooms pull toward light. Cold rooms pull toward heat and food. Filthy counters beat clean windowsills.
  3. Use the glass. Hold the magnifier to peer closer — flies hide in the clutter.
  4. Tap Get a hint and the fly lifts off. Track the flight path, then find where it settles.
  5. Don’t dawdle. Take too long and it replicates. Swarm = timeout.

How tough?

Difficulty changes the fly’s size and speed, how cold the room runs, and how fast the swarm starts.
