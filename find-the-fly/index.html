How you end it

  1. Scan the rot. Sticky counters, open jam, lamp heat, trash breath — that’s where it sits.
  2. Think like vermin. Cold rooms → radiators & food. Warm rooms → light & sugar.
  3. Use the glass. Magnify the grease. It’s smaller than your dignity.
  4. Hint = track the flight. It lifts. You watch. You remember where it lands.
  5. Spot it, then SMACK. Finding it only starts the clock. Line up the swatter and crush it before the seconds die.
  6. Hesitate and it breeds. Replicas. Swarm. You lose the room.

How bad is it?

Harder = tinier fly, faster skitter, colder room, shorter smack window, earlier swarm.
